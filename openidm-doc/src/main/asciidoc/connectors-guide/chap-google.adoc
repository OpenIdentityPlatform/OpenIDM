== Google Apps Connector (Historical)

anchor:google-connector-config[]
The historical Google Apps connector is outside the current OpenIDM distribution and the Java OpenICF release. Current OpenIDM packages do not include `provisioner.openicf-google.json` or a Google connector JAR.

If you maintain an older deployment, obtain its matching connector and configuration from your original distribution. Review the OAuth client settings against the link:https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2[Google OAuth documentation] and verify supported Admin SDK operations before upgrading.

See xref:../samples-guide/chap-google-sample.adoc#chap-google-sample[Google Apps sample status].
